# Data, images and provenance

- `data/characters.json`, `data/series.json` and `data/forms.json` are transcribed from the **user-supplied Ukiyora research PDF dated 2026-11-13**. They are unverified research entries, not canon fact assertions.
- Only names and candidate forms appear in the seed; the seed includes **no character artwork**. The Worker returns Ukiyora-branded generated initials placeholders until a rights-reviewed image URL is attached.
- Character names, likenesses, anime titles and third-party artwork may have intellectual-property restrictions. You are responsible for verifying the right to use or rehost images before updating `portrait_status` to `approved`.
- The optional `import_external_ccby.py` can accept **fadhilakbar/anime-characters-dataset** CSV (listed as CC BY 4.0). Include full source credit, link to license and record changes. The source dataset's license does not guarantee that third-party images or every upstream datum can be freely reused.
- Do **not** bulk collect from AniList. Its API terms prohibit unauthorized hoarding and storage. Do **not** rely on the discontinued public Jikan API.
- External character IDs are discovery aids, not evidence of forms, power levels, or battle outcomes.
